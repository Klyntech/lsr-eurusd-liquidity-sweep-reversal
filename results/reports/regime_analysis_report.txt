  9. SYNTHESIS AND NEXT RESEARCH QUESTIONS
======================================================================

  The data tells us:

  1. THE SWEEP-REVERSAL PHENOMENON IS REAL
     - 83/400 trades (20.8%) reach 1.5 ATR target
     - 138/400 trades (34.5%) time out with net POSITIVE P&L
     - Combined: over 55% of trades move in the predicted direction

  2. THE EDGE IS DESTROYED BY STOP LOSSES, NOT BY BAD ENTRIES
     - Stop losses account for 44.8% of trades
     - Stop loss P&L is consistently negative across all years
     - The strategy correctly identifies sweep points but the
       1.0 ATR stop is too tight for the noise level

  3. SESSION MATTERS
     - Best:  NY (PF 0.85)
     - Worst: Asian (PF 0.41)

  4. VOLATILITY REGIME MATTERS
     - Best vol regime: high_vol (90-100) (PF 0.85)

  5. SWEEP DEPTH MATTERS
     - Best: huge (2.0+) (PF 4.66)


  NEXT STEPS (no parameter changes yet):
  a) Filter to 2021-2022 trades only and study what the market was doing
     (rate regime, vol regime, central bank policy)
  b) Test: does restricting to best session improve PF?
  c) Test: does restricting to best vol regime improve PF?
  d) Test: does sweep depth filter improve PF?
  e) ALL of these are regime FILTERS, not parameter optimizations.
     They test whether the edge exists conditionally -- which is the
