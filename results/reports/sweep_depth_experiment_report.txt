========================================================================
  METHODOLOGY NOTE
========================================================================

  These experiments test a single causal variable (sweep depth)
  identified from regime analysis. They do NOT optimize parameters.

  Experiment A asks: are shallow sweeps noise, not signal?
    - If PF crosses 1.0 with filter, the hypothesis is supported.
    - If PF stays below 1.0, the sweep-depth relationship may be
      a descriptive artifact, not an exploitable filter.

  Experiment B asks: given filtered signals, what's the right
    way to harvest the reversal? This tests exit logic,
    not entry logic. The time-exit data (+2.64 pips avg)
    suggests the current 1.5 ATR target may be too ambitious.
